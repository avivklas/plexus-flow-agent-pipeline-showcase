---
id: KB-104
title: Repeat refund requests
category: refund_abuse
auto_refund_cap: 0
---
Customers with more than 3 refunds in the last 90 days must be reviewed by a human before
any further refund is finalised. Agents should still prepare a resolution, but a support lead
makes the final decision. Look for patterns such as repeated "damaged on arrival" claims for
high-value electronics.
