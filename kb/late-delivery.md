---
id: KB-102
title: Late or delayed deliveries
category: late_delivery
refund_pct: 0
refund_shipping: true
auto_refund_cap: 25
---
When an order is delivered more than 5 days after the promised delivery date, or the tracking
shows it is stuck in transit, refund the shipping fee in full. Agents may refund shipping fees
automatically up to $25.

Do not refund the item price for a late delivery unless the customer cancels the order before
it arrives. Share the latest tracking update and an honest new delivery estimate.
