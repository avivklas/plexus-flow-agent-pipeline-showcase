---
id: KB-103
title: Double charges and billing errors
category: billing_error
refund_pct: 100
refund_duplicate_only: true
auto_refund_cap: 200
---
If a customer was charged twice for the same order, refund the duplicate charge in full.
Agents may refund verified duplicate charges automatically up to $200.

Only the duplicate charge is refunded; the original order stays active. Do not offer
additional credits, free months, or extra refunds on top of the duplicate charge without
approval from a billing supervisor.
