---
id: KB-101
title: Items damaged on arrival
category: damaged_item
refund_pct: 25
auto_refund_cap: 50
---
If a customer reports that an item arrived damaged (cracked, broken, dented, scratched,
leaking), apologise and offer a goodwill partial refund of 25% of the item price without
requiring a return. Agents may issue goodwill refunds automatically up to $50.

A full refund is only available once the item has been returned and inspected. Do not promise
a free replacement in the same message as a goodwill refund; replacements are handled by the
returns team after inspection.
